Limitations

Checks are structural only. They confirm the file is a readable archive with a manifest that follows the expected format. They cannot confirm that a pack renders correctly or works with your Minecraft version.

Privacy

Pack files are read on your device and never uploaded. History is stored on this device only, and clearing your browser or app data removes it.
